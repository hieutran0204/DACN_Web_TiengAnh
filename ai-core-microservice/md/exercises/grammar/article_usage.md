# English Article System (A, An, The)

## Definite vs Indefinite Articles
Use 'a/an' (indefinite) when talking about something for the first time or when it doesn't matter which specific thing you mean. 
Use 'the' (definite) when referring to a specific item that both the writer and reader know about.

## Zero Article
Do not use articles with plural countable nouns or uncountable nouns when making general statements.
Incorrect: "The technology is very important today."
Correct: "Technology is very important today."

## Common Article Error Patterns
Many non-native speakers omit articles entirely.
Incorrect: "Student must wear uniform."
Correct: "A student must wear a uniform." or "Students must wear uniforms."
