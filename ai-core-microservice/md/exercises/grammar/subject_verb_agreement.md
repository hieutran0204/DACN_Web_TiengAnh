# Subject-Verb Agreement (SVA)

## Basic Rules
The golden rule of Subject-Verb Agreement is that a singular subject takes a singular verb, and a plural subject takes a plural verb. 
For example: "The student writes an essay" (Singular) vs "The students write an essay" (Plural).

## Common Mistakes in IELTS
Students often make mistakes when there are prepositional phrases between the subject and the verb.
Incorrect: "The quality of the students' essays are poor."
Correct: "The quality of the students' essays is poor." (The subject is 'quality', not 'essays').

## Practice Exercise
Fix the following sentence: "The number of people who uses smartphones have increased."
Correction: "The number of people who use smartphones has increased."
