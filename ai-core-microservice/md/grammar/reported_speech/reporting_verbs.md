# ĐỘNG TỪ TƯỜNG THUẬT NÂNG CAO (ADVANCED REPORTING VERBS)

Thay vì chỉ dùng *said* hoặc *told*, hãy sử dụng các động từ này để AI chấm điểm cao về "Lexical Resource".

## 1. DẠNG V-ING (REPORTING VERB + V-ING)
Dùng cho các hành động: *suggest, deny, admit, apologize for, insist on, congratulate on, accuse of...*
*   **Suggest:** He suggested **going** to the cinema.
*   **Deny:** She denied **stealing** the money.
*   **Accuse:** He accused her **of breaking** the vase.
*   **Apologize:** I apologized **for being** late.

## 2. DẠNG TO-INFINITIVE (REPORTING VERB + TO V)
Dùng cho các hành động: *promise, agree, refuse, offer, threaten, encourage, remind, warn...*
*   **Promise:** He promised **to help** me.
*   **Refuse:** She refused **to sign** the contract.
*   **Remind:** He reminded me **to lock** the door.
*   **Warn:** They warned us **not to go** out at night.

## 3. DẠNG THAT-CLAUSE
*   **Explain:** He explained **that** the train was late.
*   **Claim:** She claimed **that** she had never seen him before.
