# MẪU CHUYỂN ĐỔI GIÁN TIẾP (TRANSFORMATION TEMPLATES)

Sử dụng các mẫu này để AI gợi ý người dùng nâng cấp từ cách tường thuật đơn giản sang cách tường thuật học thuật (Advanced Reporting).

## 1. BASIC -> ACADEMIC REPORTING (Band 7.0+)
*   **Source:** `He said that he didn't do it.`
*   **Template:** `He denied + V-ing`
*   **Ví dụ:**
    *   *Input:* The suspect said he didn't steal the money.
    *   *Transformation:* The suspect **denied stealing** the money. (Ngắn gọn và học thuật hơn).

## 2. SUGGESTION UPGRADE
*   **Source:** `He said: "Why don't we go out?"`
*   **Template:** `He suggested + V-ing / He suggested that we [should] + V1`
*   **Ví dụ:**
    *   *Input:* My teacher said: "You should read more books."
    *   *Transformation:* My teacher **suggested reading** more books / **suggested that** I should read more books.

## 3. COMPLAINT -> EMPHATIC REPORTING
*   **Source:** `He said that the service was very bad.`
*   **Template:** `He complained about + Noun/V-ing`
*   **Ví dụ:**
    *   *Input:* The customers said that the food was cold.
    *   *Transformation:* The customers **complained about** the cold food.

## 4. REPORTING RESEARCH (IELTS Writing Task 2)
*   **Template:** `Researchers + [claim/assert/maintain] + that + Clause`
*   **Ví dụ:**
    *   *Input:* Experts say that global warming is dangerous.
    *   *Transformation:* Experts **assert that** global warming poses a significant threat.
