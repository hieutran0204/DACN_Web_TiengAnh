# Coherence: Logical Flow and Idea Development

## 1. Definition
Coherence refers to the "internal logic" of the writing. It is the clarity of expression and the way ideas are organized and integrated so that the reader can easily follow the writer's train of thought.

## 2. Structural Coherence (The Macro Level)
A coherent essay must follow a predictable and logical structure:
* **Progression:** The argument should move forward clearly from the Introduction to the Conclusion.
* **Paragraph Unity:** Each paragraph must focus on one single central idea (The "One-Idea-Per-Paragraph" rule).
* **Topic Sentences:** Every body paragraph must start with a clear sentence that summarizes the main point of that paragraph.

## 3. Logical Development (The Micro Level)
How ideas are expanded within a paragraph using the **PEEL** method:
1. **Point (Statement):** State the main point clearly.
2. **Explanation:** Elaborate on "Why" or "How" this point is true.
3. **Evidence/Example:** Provide a concrete example to ground the theoretical point.
4. **Link (Result/Conclusion):** Briefly link the point back to the overall essay prompt.

## 5. Common Coherence Issues & Correction Examples

### A. Irrelevant Ideas
* **Definition:** Including sentences that do not support the topic sentence or the main argument.
* **❌ Incorrect (Band 5.0):** Global warming is causing serious environmental issues. For example, many animals are losing their habitats. **Also, people should eat less meat to stay healthy.**
* **✅ Corrected (Band 7.0+):** Global warming is causing serious environmental issues. For example, many animals are losing their habitats. **Consequently, this leads to a significant decrease in biodiversity and disrupts global food chains.**

### B. Lack of Supporting Evidence
* **Definition:** Making a claim without explaining "Why" or "How" (Jumping to conclusions).
* **❌ Incorrect (Band 5.0):** Using public transport is good for the environment. **Therefore, everyone should use buses instead of cars.**
* **✅ Corrected (Band 7.0+):** Using public transport is good for the environment. **This is because buses and trains can carry more passengers at once, which significantly reduces the total carbon emissions per person.** Therefore, everyone should use buses instead of cars.

### C. Circular Reasoning
* **Definition:** Repeating the same point using different words without adding any new information.
* **❌ Incorrect (Band 5.0):** Learning English is very important. **It is essential because English is a necessary language to know.**
* **✅ Corrected (Band 7.0+):** Learning English is very important. **This is because it serves as a global lingua franca, allowing people to access international career opportunities and scientific research.**

### D. Sudden Logic Jumps
* **Definition:** Moving from point A to point C without explaining the intermediate step B.
* **❌ Incorrect (Band 5.0):** Many students use smartphones in class. **As a result, they fail their exams.**
* **✅ Corrected (Band 7.0+):** Many students use smartphones in class. **These devices often provide constant distractions, making it difficult for students to focus on the lesson.** As a result, their academic performance suffers, often leading to failing grades.
